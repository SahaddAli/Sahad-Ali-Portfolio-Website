Dissertation project

StudyMate: an AI study assistant students actually trust

Problem
Students were using general chatbots for revision, but 6 in 10 I surveyed didn't trust the answers enough to rely on them.
What I did
Ran 18 user interviews, wrote the product requirements, and prototyped a source-citing assistant built on course materials.
Outcome
Trust scores rose from 2.9 to 4.3 out of 5 in usability testing with 24 students.

How I take an idea from problem to product

  1. 1

    Discover

    Talk to users before touching a model. Find the job they're hiring the product to do.

  2. 2

    Define

    Write the problem down, decide what success looks like, and agree on what we won't build.

  3. 3

    Prototype

    Build the cheapest version that answers the riskiest question, often with no-code tools and an LLM.

  4. 4

    Measure

    Test with real people, check where the AI gets things wrong, and decide whether to keep going.
